About Us

Handles is a creative film and visual storytelling company.

We create videos and visual content for brands, businesses, and institutions. But we are not just a regular video production company.

Our entire creative and production team comes from a professional film background — including directors, cinematographers, editors, and seasoned film technicians. Because of this, we bring the thinking, storytelling, visual quality, and production approach of cinema into commercial and brand content.

About the Founders

The creative and strategic leadership driving Handles across India and the UAE.

M.S. Hari

Founder & Director

M.S. Hari is the Founder and Director of Handles, with over a decade of experience in the film, advertising, and video production industry. Having worked across direction, creative development, and production, he has been part of projects for leading brands including Amazon, Flipkart, Lenskart, Finolex, Max Fashion, and GRT. With a strong passion for visual communication, Hari founded Handles to bring filmmaking quality and creative thinking under one roof.

Animesh Sen

Co-Founder | Brand & Business Dev

Animesh has spent his career understanding what makes brands connect with people. From building client relationships and developing brand opportunities to shaping ideas into real-world projects, he brings a strong mix of business thinking and creative curiosity to Handles. He works closely with brands, creators, and production teams to discover stories worth telling and turn them into content people remember.

Caroline Daniel

Co-Founder | UAE Operations & Client Relations

Caroline Daniel is part of Handles’ UAE team, helping build the company’s presence and connect with clients across the region. Based in the UAE, she looks after client communication, project coordination, and local business operations, working closely with the creative team in India to deliver seamless execution and quality visual content tailored to client needs.
